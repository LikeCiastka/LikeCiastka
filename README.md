## 🚁 Research: UxV × AI × Anomaly Detection

My Master's research focuses on **cybersecurity of unmanned vehicles
(UAV/UGV)**, with particular interest in **telemetry analysis,
anomaly detection, and machine learning-based identification of
suspicious behaviour**.

The idea is not simply to ask:

> **"Is this data abnormal?"**

but:

> **"Is this abnormal behaviour meaningful from a security perspective?"**

That means looking at **telemetry, temporal behaviour, anomaly scores,
and cybersecurity context** rather than treating every outlier as an attack.
